Flight Horizon

Flight Horizon is one of my first projects I've made with a team. The project is mainly focused on realistic flight physics and player-to-player RPG-style communication. It was inspired by PTFS (Pilot training flight simulator). There I've learned basics of system design within Roblox Studio, Roblox's vehicle framework, Datastores and UI.

Details / Features (Implemented by Me):
  • Flight Physics: Calculate forces based on vehicle's shape.
  • Flgiht Control: A way to control the flight by moving specific parts of the aircraft.
  • Dynamic Lighting System: Realistic day/night cycles and environmental lighting that enhance immersion.
  • Daily Rewards System: track last claim time (using datastores) and show configured reward previews.
  • Enhanced Chat Communication: Role-based coloring, groups and radio-like announcements.
